Are dogs allowed on most South Florida beaches?

No. On most public beaches in Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach, dogs (other than service animals) are not allowed. Use a designated dog beach.

  • Miami Beach: dogs are prohibited from city beaches except the designated Bark Beach at 80 to 81 Streets [source].
  • Palm Beach County: dogs are not allowed on county beaches, with the exception of ADA service animals; Jupiter Dog Beach is the county's off-leash beach stretch [source, source 2].
  • Fort Lauderdale: pets are not allowed on beaches or in any body of water unless permitted by law, except at Canine Beach [source].
  • Jupiter calls its beach "one of the few remaining dog-friendly beaches in the state of Florida" [source].

Where dogs are allowed, rules usually include a leash to and from the sand, a current rabies tag or vaccination proof, a limit of two or three dogs per person, and picking up waste. See the dog beach list. County license fees, where to get the tag, and which places ask for proof are on the license and rabies page.
