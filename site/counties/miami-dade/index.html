Haulover Park Dog Beach (Miami-Dade County)
Miami-Dade		Haulover Park, 10800 Collins Ave., between lifeguard towers 2 and 3; access via parking lots 3 and 4Daily, sunrise to sunset (official park page).Park parking: Monday to Thursday $7 per car; weekends and holidays $10 per car (park page). Parking lots open sunrise until one hour before sunset. [official page, source 2]
Check Conflict: the official park page says daily sunrise to sunset, while a local news item (Miami Laker, not official) describes a 'Dogs on the Dunes' schedule of Wednesdays, Saturdays and Sundays, 8 a.m. to 3 p.m. Call the park before going.
