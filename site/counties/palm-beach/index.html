Palm Beach		3001 N State Road A1A, Boca Raton, FL 33487, between lifeguard towers 18 and 207 days a week: 7 a.m. to 9 a.m., and 3 p.m. to sunset during Eastern Standard Time or 5 p.m. to sunset during Daylight Saving Time.Bark Beach Dog Entry Permit required (Oct 1 to Sep 30).
