Bark Beach at Spanish River Park (Boca Raton)
Palm Beach		3001 N State Road A1A, Boca Raton, FL 33487, between lifeguard towers 18 and 207 days a week. 3 p.m. to sunset during Eastern Standard Time; 5 p.m. to sunset during Daylight Saving Time.Bark Beach Dog Entry Permit required (Oct 1 to Sep 30). Annual resident (City of Boca Raton and Greater Boca Raton Beach and Park District) $70; annual non-resident $630; three-day permit $20 per visit, used on three consecutive days.
