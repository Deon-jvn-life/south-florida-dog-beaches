Dog beaches

  • All dog beaches7 designated dog beaches in Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach, with days, seasonal hours, permits, fees and rules from official pages.
  • North Beach Bark BeachNorth Beach Bark Beach, Miami Beach: Friday to Sunday, 3 p.m. to 7 p.m. March to October and 1:30 p.m. to 5:30 p.m. November to February. No permit stated.
  • Haulover Dog BeachHaulover Park Dog Beach: daily sunrise to sunset between lifeguard towers 2 and 3. Parking is $7 Monday to Thursday and $10 on weekends and holidays.
  • Hollywood Dog BeachDog Beach at Hollywood Beach: sunrise to sunset, no passes required, between Pershing and Custer Street lifeguard stands. Dogs need a current rabies tag.
  • Canine BeachCanine Beach in Fort Lauderdale: 6 a.m. to 9 a.m. and 5 p.m. to 7 p.m. North of Sunrise Boulevard on A1A. No permit stated on the official page.
  • Wags and WavesWags and Waves Dog Beach at the SE 2nd Street Beach Access in Pompano Beach. Hours and fees were not stated on the official pages we could read.
  • Boca Bark BeachBark Beach at Spanish River Park: 7 days a week, 7 a.m. to 9 a.m., then 3 p.m. to sunset in standard time or 5 p.m. to sunset in daylight time. Permit required.
  • Jupiter Dog BeachJupiter Dog Beach, Palm Beach County: sunrise to sunset, no permit stated, and parking is free. Leash dogs from the car to the beach.

Miami-Dade

  • Miami-Dade CountyMiami-Dade beaches and parks: North Beach Bark Beach, Haulover Dog Beach, Haulover Bark Park, West Kendall, Amelia Earhart, Greynolds, Tropical and Lago Mar.
  • Haulover Bark ParkHaulover Park Bark Park: sunrise to sunset, seven days a week. Parking is $5.47 per vehicle with the dog in the car. Separate small and large dog areas.
  • West Kendall dog parkWest Kendall District Park dog park is temporarily closed starting Oct. 14. Tentative reopening is Friday, Oct. 30. The park page states no year and no fee.
  • Amelia Earhart dog parkAmelia Earhart Park bark park admission is free. Parking is free Monday to Thursday and $8 Friday to Sunday and holidays. Bark park hours are not stated.
  • East Greynolds dog parkEast Greynolds Park dog park lists no separate entry fee. Parking is free Monday to Thursday and $10 Friday to Sunday. Bark park hours are not stated.
  • Tropical Park dog parkTropical Park dog park at 7900 SW 40 St., Miami. Hours and fees are not stated on the county dog parks page. County dog park rules apply.
  • Lago Mar dog runLago Mar Park dog run, 8165 SW 162 Ave., Miami. Hours and fees are not stated. No water, bag dispensers or waste baskets; bring your own.

Broward

  • Broward CountyBroward beaches and parks: Hollywood Dog Beach, Canine Beach, Wags and Waves, Barkham, Woofing Waters, Holiday Park, Riverside, Snyder and Canine Corner.
  • Barkham at MarkhamBarkham at Markham Park, Sunrise: closed Tuesday mornings until 10 a.m. Annual membership $25 or $5 daily, up to three dogs. Small and large dog areas.
  • Woofing WatersWoofing Waters at Quiet Waters Park: 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. daily. Annual fee $25 or $5 daily. Closed third week of January through second week of April.
  • Holiday Park dog parkHoliday Park Dog Park in Fort Lauderdale: 7:30 a.m. to 7 p.m., closed Monday and Wednesday 8 a.m. to 11 a.m. for maintenance. Up to three dogs per person.
  • Riverside dog parkRiverside Neighborhood Dog Park in Fort Lauderdale: 6 a.m. to 9 p.m. Fee not stated. Rules include a license, three dogs max and no puppies under four months.
  • Snyder Park bark parkSnyder Park Bark Park, Fort Lauderdale: 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. Closed Tuesday and Thursday 8 to 11 a.m. for maintenance, and on Christmas Day and New Year's Day.
  • Canine CornerCanine Corner Dog Park in Pompano Beach: daily 6:30 a.m. to 8:30 p.m., closed Tuesdays. Members only. Separate areas under and over 25 pounds.

Palm Beach

  • Palm Beach CountyPalm Beach County dog beaches and parks, including Jupiter Dog Beach, Boca Raton Bark Beach, Canine Cove, Lake Ida, Lake Woof and Pooch Pines, with hours.
  • Canine CoveCanine Cove at Burt Aaronson South County Regional Park: sunrise to sunset, closed Wednesday 12 p.m. to 3 p.m. for maintenance. No fee stated.
  • Lake Ida dog parkDog Park at Lake Ida West Park, 1455 Lake Ida Road, Delray Beach: sunrise to sunset, closed Thursday 12 p.m. to 3 p.m. No fee stated on the county page.
  • Lake WoofLake Woof at John Prince Memorial Park, 2520 Lake Worth Road: sunrise to sunset, closed Thursday 12 p.m. to 3 p.m. No entry fee stated.
  • Pooch PinesPooch Pines at Okeeheelee Park, 7715 Forest Hill Boulevard: sunrise to sunset, closed Wednesday 12 p.m. to 3 p.m. No fee stated on the county page.
