Barkham at Markham Park (Broward County)

Type: Off-leash dog park, Broward County

Location: 16001 W. State Rd. 84, Sunrise 33326

Days and hours: Park's normal operating hours; closed every Tuesday morning until 10 a.m. for maintenance.

Permit, fee, parking: Annual membership $25 (up to three dogs, one access card); daily fee $5 (up to three dogs); extra access card $15; replacement access card $5. A dog special event session is $5 for a member and $6.50 for a non-member. Proof of current rabies vaccination with expiration or administered date required.

Phone: 954-357-8868 (Markham Park)

[official page, source 2]

Seasonal: the county fee schedule says Barkham and Woofing Waters close from the third week of January through the second week of April for maintenance.

Rules

  • Separate small dog (up to 25 pounds) and large dog areas.
  • Owners clean up after their pets.
