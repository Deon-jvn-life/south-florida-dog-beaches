Bark Beach at Spanish River Park (Boca Raton)

Type: Dog beach, Palm Beach County

Location: 3001 N State Road A1A, Boca Raton, FL 33487, between lifeguard towers 18 and 20

Days and hours: 7 days a week. 3 p.m. to sunset during Eastern Standard Time; 5 p.m. to sunset during Daylight Saving Time.

Permit, fee, parking: Bark Beach Dog Entry Permit required (Oct 1 to Sep 30). Annual resident (City of Boca Raton and Greater Boca Raton Beach and Park District) $70; annual non-resident $630; three-day permit $20 per visit, used on three consecutive days. Fees per household as of October 1, 2026. Beach parking is not included. From October 1, 2026, gatehouses no longer accept cash.

Phone: (561) 393-7815 (Park Ranger Headquarters, incidents)

[official page]

Rules

  • Carry the permit at all times; each dog needs its own permit; limit of two dogs per person.
  • Leash dogs when entering and leaving; off leash only in the dog area during Bark Beach hours.
  • Carry a waste disposal device; dogs must not dig.
  • No nuisance behavior such as excessive barking or aggression.
