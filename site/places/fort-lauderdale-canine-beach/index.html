Canine Beach (Fort Lauderdale)

Type: Dog beach, Broward County

Location: Second set of parking spaces north of Sunrise Boulevard on A1A (approximately 1269 N. Fort Lauderdale Beach Blvd)

Days and hours: 6 a.m. to 9 a.m. and 5 p.m. to 7 p.m. (official Dog-Friendly Places page).

Permit, fee, parking: No permit or fee is stated on the official page we could read.

[official page]

Conflict and gap: the Canine Beach URL provided (fortlauderdale.gov .../beach-public-canine-beach) returned 404 on 21 Oct 2026; facts are from the city's Dog-Friendly Places page. Community sites (not official) still describe Friday to Sunday hours (3 p.m. to 7 p.m. November to March, 3 p.m. to 9 p.m. April to October) and a permit of $30 resident, $45 non-resident or $7 weekend. Confirm with Fort Lauderdale Parks before you go.

Rules

  • Dogs only from lifeguard tower 16 to a point 100 yards north of Sunrise Boulevard (posted as Canine Beach).
  • Maximum of three dogs per owner or handler.
  • A1A dog walking (west side of A1A, Sunrise Blvd south to Seabreeze Blvd): 6 a.m. to 9 a.m. and 5 p.m. to 7 p.m.; west side of A1A north of Sunrise Blvd: 24 hours.
