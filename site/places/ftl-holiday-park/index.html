Holiday Park Dog Park (Fort Lauderdale)

Type: Off-leash dog park, Broward County

Location: Holiday Park, Fort Lauderdale (street address not given on the dog page)

Days and hours: 7:30 a.m. to 7 p.m.; closed Monday and Wednesday, 8 a.m. to 11 a.m., for maintenance.

Permit, fee, parking: Not stated.

[official page]

Rules

  • Current vaccination and license; no puppies under four months.
  • No more than three dogs per person; no digging; keep the gate closed.
  • No dogs in heat, ill or with parasites. Not staffed; use at your own risk.
