Dog Beach at Hollywood Beach

Type: Dog beach, Broward County

Location: 4999 N Surf Rd, Hollywood, FL 33020, between the Pershing and Custer Street lifeguard stands

Days and hours: Sunrise to sunset (official page; no day restriction stated).

Permit, fee, parking: No passes required. Street parking.

Phone: 954-921-3404 (City of Hollywood number as listed by Broward County)

[official page, source 2]

Older community guides describe weekend-only hours and a permit; the current official page says sunrise to sunset with no passes required.

Rules

  • Owners are legally responsible for and must control their dogs at all times, and clean up waste.
  • Dogs must have a current rabies tag.
  • Dogs must be leashed anywhere outside Dog Beach.
  • Prohibited: sick dogs, dogs in heat, aggressive dogs, other animals.
  • Exit the beach immediately when lightning is seen or thunder heard.
