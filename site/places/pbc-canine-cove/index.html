Canine Cove at Burt Aaronson South County Regional Park

Type: Off-leash dog park, Palm Beach County

Location: 12551 Glades Road, west of Boca Raton

Days and hours: Sunrise to sunset; closed every Wednesday 12 p.m. to 3 p.m. for maintenance.

Permit, fee, parking: No fee stated on the county page.

[official page, source 2]

Rules

  • Palm Beach County dog park rules: limit 2 dogs per person, leash in hand at all times, dogs vaccinated and licensed, no food, no dogs in heat, under voice control.
  • Small dog area for dogs under 30 pounds.
