Canine Corner Dog Park (Pompano Beach)

Type: Off-leash dog park, Broward County

Location: 1101 N. Federal Highway, Pompano Beach, FL 33062 (Federal Hwy and NE 10th Street)

Days and hours: Daily 6:30 a.m. to 8:30 p.m.; closed Tuesdays for maintenance.

Permit, fee, parking: Members only. Annual: resident $32 per dog ($10 each additional), non-resident $64 ($20). Six months: resident $22 ($10), non-resident $44 ($20). Weekly and daily options also offered. Register with proof of residency, rabies certificate (tag alone not enough) and a dog photo.

Phone: (954) 786-4574 (Dog Park Hotline)

[official page]

Rules

  • Separate areas for dogs under 25 pounds and 25 pounds and over.
  • Maximum three dogs per adult; 6-foot leash, no retractable leashes; no spiked, choke or prong collars.
  • Current rabies tag and personalized ID tag on the collar; no infants, toddlers or strollers.
